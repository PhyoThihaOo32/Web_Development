The Yurts at Pacific Trails

Pacific Trails Resortoffers:

What is a yurt?
A yurt is a round, tent-like structure that provides comfortable shelter while allowing guests to enjoy nature.
How are the yurts furnished?
The yurts include beds, seating, electricity, and basic furniture for a relaxing and convenient stay.
What should I bring?
Guests should bring personal items, comfortable clothing, hiking shoes, and any special supplies they may need.

Top Three Activities To Do at Mandalay

Watch Sunset at U Bein Bridge

The world’s longest teakwood bridge, offering breathtaking sunset views and peaceful walks.

Explore Shwenandaw Monastery

A masterpiece of traditional Burmese teak architecture and intricate carvings.

Maha Myat Muni Pagoda

One of Mandalay’s holiest sites, home to the revered gold-covered Buddha statue.

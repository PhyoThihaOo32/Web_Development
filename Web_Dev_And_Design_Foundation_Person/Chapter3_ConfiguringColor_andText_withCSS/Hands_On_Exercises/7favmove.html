The Shawshank Redemption

The Shawshank Redemption (1994) is a prison drama about Andy Dufresne, a banker wrongly convicted of murder, who forms a deep friendship with Ellis “Red” Redding while finding hope and purpose during decades at Shawshank State Penitentiary. The film explores resilience, justice, and the power of hope.
